# Sustained DCO comparison on a virtual link

The two-vCPU AMD EPYC Rome KVM lab ran Linux `7.0.0-44-generic`,
mainline `ovpn`, packaged OpenVPN `2.7.0-1ubuntu1.4`
(executable SHA-256
`06d101129eec1816fe2f79910c292a33da2779e06e073dbc4de295ad6dc93ff6`)
and iperf3 3.20. Both endpoints ran the same pinned Community binary.
They used verified TLS and AES-256-GCM with a 15-second rekey interval.
The outer transport was UDP across a veth pair joining two disposable
network namespaces. The inner workload used two concurrent TCP
streams in each direction for 30 seconds, with 256- or 1,200-byte
configured TCP MSS. ICMP probes ran during traffic. CPU includes both
endpoints, traffic generators and the guest kernel, from busy ticks
in `/proc/stat`. It excludes CPU steal time. The reported goodput sums
received bytes in both directions.

| Backend | Configured MSS | Repeat | Goodput (Mbit/s) | Guest CPU s/Gbit | ICMP p99 (ms) | ICMP received / sent | TCP retransmissions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Userspace | 256 | 1 | 123.55 | 14.842 | 14.00 | 282 / 298 | 5,802 |
| Userspace | 256 | 2 | 118.91 | 15.453 | 14.70 | 283 / 298 | 5,256 |
| DCO | 256 | 1 | 1,000.16 | 1.999 | 0.387 | 289 / 289 | 0 |
| DCO | 256 | 2 | 998.61 | 2.003 | 0.340 | 289 / 289 | 114 |
| Userspace | 1,200 | 1 | 469.86 | 3.832 | 9.56 | 298 / 298 | 7,516 |
| Userspace | 1,200 | 2 | 487.79 | 3.743 | 8.41 | 295 / 298 | 6,804 |
| DCO | 1,200 | 1 | 3,499.71 | 0.572 | 0.060 | 289 / 289 | 17 |
| DCO | 1,200 | 2 | 4,617.03 | 0.433 | 0.046 | 289 / 289 | 9 |

The runner verified `tun` links in the userspace cases and `ovpn` links
on both sides in the DCO cases. All eight runs carried tunnel traffic
and deleted both links after normal shutdown. The sustained logs
recorded TLS rekey. The shorter three-second run also passed all eight
cases as a setup and cleanup check.

The large difference justifies a physical-lab investigation. Link
type, socket protocol, key format, driver ABI and cipher must be
qualified before changing the project backend. The VM exposes a
virtual NIC and no measured 1/10 GbE path. Established-connection
recovery, RSS attribution, crash rollback and other cipher and
framing combinations remain unqualified. [Backend decision](../adr/0008-linux-data-offload.md)

## Reproduce

Use a Linux kernel with working mainline `ovpn`, a pinned compatible
OpenVPN test binary, iproute2, iperf3, OpenSSL and Python 3. The runner
creates synthetic certificates and isolated network namespaces with
passwordless sudo. It removes the synthetic keys when it exits. Keep
the report directory private and outside the checkout.

~~~sh
OPENVPN_DCO_SECONDS=30 bash tests/interop/dco-lab.sh "$PEER" "$NEW_REPORT_DIR"
~~~
